# FSCore - Player Guide

You are joining a game or a server that runs FSCore. Here is what to expect and how to find your way around.

## Your first session

1. **Create your character.** The identity screen asks for a first and last name, a date of birth and a specialization (crop farmer, rancher, forester, mechanic, trucker or business owner). You also read the server's house rules here. On a server, the server name is shown at the top.
2. **Meet your phone.** Press **/** (slash, or the numpad divide key) to open it. A short tour on the first start points you to it.
3. **Open the Journey app.** It is pinned for new players: a first-day checklist with a reward for each step, from reading the welcome board to your first job, your first catch and joining a farm. Each step has a "Take me there" button that sets a map marker.
4. **Earn your first money.** You start with a small cash amount. Mini jobs (deliveries and junk cleanup in the Giggit app), fishing and scavenging get you going until a farm hires you.

## The phone

| App | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Journey** | Your first-day checklist and rewards (new players) |
| **Messages**, **Contacts**, **HamGram**, **FarmFeed** | Talk to other players, share photos and posts |
| **Finances** | Wallet, bank accounts, loans, credit score, bills and invoices, taxes, payroll |
| **Giggit** | Delivery and cleanup gigs with pay that scales with distance and cargo |
| **Farm Manager** | Your farm's team, hiring, permissions and activity (owners and managers) |
| **Identity** | Your ID card, licenses and training |
| **Keys** | Vehicle keys and who may drive what |
| **Inventory** | What you carry, weight and sub-slots |
| **Crafting** | Recipes and crafting stations |
| **Needs** | Hunger and thirst |
| **Shops** | Stores and fuel stations |
| **Marketplace** | Buy, sell and rent between players and farms |
| **Garage** | Your personal garage |
| **Critter** | Your farm dog: needs, commands, bed and bowl |
| **HerdSync**, **SiloView**, **Productions** | Animals, silos and production points at a glance |

Not every app is present on every server: a module that the administrator switched off does not show its app.

## Keys to know

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/` | Open or close the phone |
| `I` | Open the inventory backpack |
| `R` | Interact with kiosks, boards, ATMs, doors and items on the ground |
| `O` | Radial menu |
| `Ctrl` + `B` | Banking menu |
| `Ctrl` + `H` | Toggle the money display |
| `Ctrl` + `J` | Jobs and payroll |
| `Delete` | Toggle the economy HUD |

All bindings can be changed in the game's input settings. The phone key is also shown in the F1 help.

## Things worth knowing

- **Money is personal and farm money is separate.** Your wallet and your bank accounts are yours. The farm account is the normal Farming Simulator account. ATMs and the banking menu move money between them.
- **Hunger and thirst** drain slowly. Eat and drink from the inventory; food comes from stores, crafting and the starter package. The HUD warns you when you run low.
- **Licenses** gate some things: a fishing license for fishing, a tractor or heavy equipment license for vehicles when the server has that rule on, a marine license for boats. Training takes game time and costs a fee; you apply from the Identity app.
- **Farm dogs** can follow you, patrol, ride along and work on farm duty, which gives animal pens a production bonus when the dog is fed and happy.
- **Reputation** grows with what you do: deliveries raise Logistics, fishing raises Angling, selling raises Business, and so on. Higher levels unlock better gigs and bonuses.
- **Your settings are yours.** Phone look, notifications, hints and the compass options are stored on your own PC, not on the server.

## Common questions

- **"I pressed / and nothing happened."** The phone is dormant while a game menu is open. Close the menu first. If a server has the phone switched off, the key does nothing.
- **"Where do I get my starter package?"** On most servers it is delivered automatically when your character is created. Otherwise walk to a welcome board and claim it there.
- **"The dog is not listening."** Dogs need food and water; a hungry dog goes to its bowl first. Check the Critter app.
- **"Can I turn off the hints and the tour?"** Yes, in the phone's Settings app.

## Getting help

Ask your server administrator first. For bugs, use the [Issues tab](https://github.com/RocklandUSA/FS25_FSCore/issues) and include the steps and your log excerpt.
