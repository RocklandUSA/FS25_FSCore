# FSCore - Player Guide

You are joining a game or a server that runs FSCore 1.0.0.0. Here is what to expect and how to find your way around.

Not everything below is on every server: each feature is a module the administrator can switch off, and a module that is off does not show its app, kiosk or key.

## Your first session

1. **Create your character.** The character creator asks for a first and last name, a date of birth and a specialisation (Crop Farmer, Rancher, Forester, Mechanic, Trucker or Business Owner). These are your in-game character's details, so make them up. Each specialisation gives a small perk, such as extra XP in its skill or a licence at half price. On a server you also read the server's name and house rules here.
2. **Meet your phone.** Press **/** (slash, or the numpad divide key) to open it.
3. **Open the Journey app.** It is pinned for new players: a first-day checklist that teaches FSCore by doing, with a small welcome grant and Community XP for each step, from reading the welcome board to your first job. Each step can set a map marker for you. Experienced players can skip it.
4. **Earn your first money.** Giggit gigs, Farm Eats deliveries, Shiftit company shifts, fishing and scavenging get you going until a farm hires you.

## Keys to know

These are the default keys from the mod's own bindings. All of them can be changed in the game's control settings.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/` (also numpad `/`) | Open or close the phone |
| `Left Alt` + `/` | End phone navigation: cancel the route and put the mounted phone away |
| `F10` or `Left Alt` + `I` | Open the backpack |
| `R` | Interact: kiosks, boards, ATMs, doors, seats, NPCs and items on the ground |
| `F9` | Radial menu: emotes, quick actions and commands for your dog |
| `F6` | Player Hub (My ID, Farm Manager, compass settings and other player tools). Administrators get the Admin Hub instead |
| `Left Ctrl` + `H` | Show or hide the money display |
| `R` in a Lawncare mowing vehicle | Show the grass still to mow |
| `N` / `M` in the tower crane cab | Turn the crane hook (and its load) left or right |

Other actions depend on where you are (building, decorating, the arcade, the crane). The prompt on screen always shows the key.

## Things worth knowing

- **Your money is yours.** Cash, your bank accounts and your loans belong to your character. The farm account is the normal Farming Simulator account. ATMs move cash in and out of the bank.
- **My ID** (Player Hub, `F6`) shows your ID card with your overall FSCore level, every licence (held, in training, or what it takes to get it) and every skill with its level and title.
- **Skills and levels.** Everything you do earns XP in nine skill trees: Agronomy, Husbandry, Forestry, Logistics, Mechanical, Business, Community, Angling and Mining. Your overall level shows as a coloured chip on your name tag and in the **Overall** tab of Competitions. Farm work by hand or with helpers also earns Farm Points, farming ranks and medals.
- **Licences.** You need a Fishing Licence to fish (sold at the general store). When the server switches Equipment Licences on, vehicles need a licence by class: cars for everyone, Ag Operator for farm machines, Truck for road trucks, Forestry for forestry machines. You apply in the phone's Identity app.
- **Homes.** Buy a house from Lizard Realty outright or with a mortgage (up to three mortgages per player). Mortgage payments come out automatically; savings cover a payment before it counts as missed. Decorate in the Home Decor app and sit in your own chairs, recliners and garden chairs. The owner of a house built from a blueprint or kit can demolish it in the Real Estate app (a fee of 10 % of its value, at least 2,500) to build again.
- **Building.** Buy a lot and the plans, set the site out, bring the pallets and build piece by piece. Concrete has to cure before the next pieces go on. When a Ranch or Harvest House is finished you pick its colour scheme at the home board.
- **Community projects.** Some town buildings are built by the whole server: the Car Dealership, the Post Office & Parcel Depot, the Self-Storage Yard and the Co-op Grain Elevator. Each one unlocks something for everyone when it is finished: FSCore's car dealership, mechanic and towing calls; the mail and parcel rounds; big storage units; the Farmers Co-op, which pays 10 % more than the best price on the map for grain. Join in at the site: every player puts in one shift a day, Town Hall pays for the work and for every pallet you haul, and the top three builders get a hammer trophy. Until a project is built, its features say what they are waiting for.
- **Hunger, thirst and fatigue** drain slowly. Eat and drink from the backpack; food goes stale, and the farm fridge keeps it fresh for longer.
- **Farm dogs** are adopted from the Pet Shelter. They can follow you, patrol, ride along and work on farm duty, which gives animal pens a bonus when the dog is fed and happy.
- **Your settings are yours.** Phone look, notifications, hints and the compass options are stored on your own PC, not on the server.

## Common questions

- **"I pressed / and nothing happened."** The phone stays closed while a game menu is open. Close the menu first. On a server with the Phone module off, the key does nothing.
- **"The dealership or the mechanic says it unlocks when the Car Dealership is built."** Your server uses the community project: help build the dealership, or ask an administrator.
- **"Where do I get my starter package?"** On many servers it is delivered when your character is created. Otherwise walk to a welcome board and claim it there.
- **"The dog is not listening."** Dogs need food and water; a hungry dog goes to its bowl first. Check the Pets app.
- **"Can I turn off the hints?"** Yes, in the phone's Settings app.
