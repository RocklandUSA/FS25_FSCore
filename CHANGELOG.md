# Changelog

All notable changes to FSCore are listed here. FSCore is distributed only through the GIANTS ModHub; a version listed here is available once GIANTS has approved and published it.

## 1.0.0.0 - first ModHub release

Submitted to GIANTS for ModHub review in November 2026 (pending approval). The first public release of FSCore - Rural Life Simulation Framework for Farming Simulator 25: one mod, 50 switchable modules, 44 phone apps, in English, German, Polish and French, for singleplayer, multiplayer and dedicated servers. Full notes: [docs/RELEASE_NOTES_1.0.0.0.md](docs/RELEASE_NOTES_1.0.0.0.md).

### Main systems

- **Phone** (`/`) with 44 apps: messages and social apps, Finances, Navigation with turn-by-turn directions, Identity, Keys, backpack, crafting, jobs, reputation, Town Hall news, and the farm apps PlotView, HerdSync, SimpliBarn, SiloView, Productions and Farm Stats
- **Character and identity:** character creator, ID card, licences and training, the My ID screen with the overall FSCore level
- **Money:** pocket cash and ATMs, current, savings and business accounts, invoices, income tax, VAT and property tax, credit rating, mortgages and dealership finance, loan details in the Finances app
- **Homes and Real Estate:** Lizard Realty homes to buy outright or with a mortgage, interiors, Home Decor with almost 300 furniture pieces and nearly 90 plants and garden pieces, seats, spare keys, house demolition for rebuilding
- **Construction:** building lots, house plans and kits (Ranch House, Harvest House), garden builds and raised-bed gardening, built stage by stage with hand tools, a colour scheme for each finished house
- **AutoPlot:** a Town Hall field becomes a planned neighbourhood with streets, street lights, lawns and lots for sale; street names and home addresses on every road
- **Community projects that unlock features:** Car Dealership (FSCore's dealership, mechanic and towing calls), Post Office & Parcel Depot (mail and parcel rounds), Self-Storage Yard (storage units), Co-op Grain Elevator (the Farmers Co-op with +10 % grain prices and massive storage, a truck scale). Town Hall shift pay, builders' plaque and hammer trophies, tower crane and man basket on the elevator, `fscUnlock` admin override, listed in Map Audit
- **Jobs:** Shiftit company shifts (mail, parcels, propane, towing, kerbside refuse, warehouse forklift, Lizard Lawncare mowing) with ranks and work clothes; Giggit gigs; Farm Eats deliveries; time-clock pay with roles, permissions and payroll
- **Progression:** nine skill trees with ten levels and titles each, leaderboards, Competitions with an overall standing, colour-coded level chips on name tags, farming ranks, medals and Farmer of the Week
- **The mine:** lift, dig sections, ore veins, pickaxe and rock drill, headlamps and lanterns, ore carts on laid track, finds, mine forge and the Mine app
- **Survival and items:** hunger, thirst and fatigue, food that goes stale, backpack, crates, lockers and farm fridges, crafting, shops and fuel stations with hand-nozzle refuelling, the F9 radial menu
- **Vehicles:** dealership with finance, vehicle keys and locks, personal and farm garages, rentals, a mechanic, equipment licences (optional)
- **Outdoors:** fishing, boating, farm dogs from the Pet Shelter, scavenging and salvaging, mushrooms, the camp chair and fire pit, the EGG RUN arcade cabinet
- **Town:** welcome boards and Town Hall, bulletin boards, a player marketplace for vehicles, the Journey first-day checklist, Seasonal Street Lights, Map Essentials
- **For server owners:** Admin Hub (`F6`) with Map Audit and every admin tool, the FSCore Settings page with 93 live settings, map layouts for the base-game maps, moderation tools, restart backups
